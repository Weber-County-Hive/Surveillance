When we published our investigation into Flock surveillance in Weber County, readers responded with questions — smart ones, fair ones, the same ones we asked ourselves when we started reporting. Below are the ten we heard most often, answered one at a time, with the sources behind each answer.

Jump to a question

    1.6 million
    Searches by federal agencies, cited in California litigation

    That figure comes from discovery in the Gibbs Mura v. Flock Safety litigation — searches run against Flock's databases by agencies including ICE and CBP. It's the same vendor system deployed in Weber County.

    Where this leaves us

    These are legitimate questions, and skepticism about a story like this one is healthy. Flock cameras are real, they're deployed in Weber County, and — as the record below shows — they're accessible to police without a documented warrant requirement. Class-action litigation elsewhere is producing evidence that unauthorized searches happen.

    What you do with that information is up to you. We'll keep filing records requests and reporting what comes back.
