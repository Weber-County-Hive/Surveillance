The Questions Residents Keep Asking

From Ohio to Utah, people who have never met are standing up at council meetings and asking Flock and their police departments the same eight questions. This page lays out each question, the answer residents were given, and what the public record shows.

How Communities Are Thinking

The same three groups show up in every town

Comments on this series and coverage of hearings elsewhere sort into the same three positions. Councils tend to change course when the middle group moves.

Yes, with limits

Fine with cameras if searches are audited, data is kept briefly, and misuse is punished.

Useful, but worried

Sees the crime-solving value; worried about sharing with other agencies, stalking cases, and who can see the data.

No mass tracking

Opposes recording every driver no matter what the policy says.

What moves the middle group

In the cases on this page it was a local fact, not national news. In Oshkosh, Wis., the police chief said his staff found information that conflicted with what the council had been told. In Apex, N.C., police found two federal agencies had been "mistakenly" given access. In Sheboygan, Wis., a Flock salesperson offered to sell the city its own traffic data. In Washington Terrace, the mayor cited the governor's concerns.

Your Turn

Bring these to a Weber County meeting

Each question below is one residents elsewhere asked without getting a direct answer. Here is who in Weber County is positioned to answer it.

Weber County Sheriff's Office
  • Which outside agencies searched our cameras last month, and how many searches did each run?
  • Is federal sharing switched off in our account?
  • What retention setting are we on today?
Weber County Commission
  • Does our Flock contract give Flock a license to use our data in "aggregated" form, and for what?
  • Has the county been offered Flock's traffic analytics product?
  • What independent numbers show the cameras' results here?
Ogden, North Ogden, Plain City councils
  • Does our city's data feed the Sheriff's network, and who can search it?
  • Will a Flock representative stay through public comment at our next hearing?
The Throughline

The questions are the same from Columbus to Washington Terrace because the answers haven't settled them. When a resident's question gets an answer to a different question, the original question is still open.
