Where one search traveled

Flock's system doesn't route a search to one department. It routes it to every network that department has been given access to — and the median department searching Weber County's cameras reaches nearly 3,000 of them at once.

Primary Takeaway

Weber County installed ten cameras. Access did not remain local.

Sources & methodology
  • Utah Civic Compact, "Ten cameras in one Utah county were searched 5.1 million times by police in 45 states" — full GRAMA production and archive: utahciviccompact.org/alpr
  • KSL, "Group cites 'striking' Weber County data on Flock cameras as reason for lawmakers to act," Daniel Woodruff, Aug. 13, 2026
  • Standard-Examiner, "Most of this Utah county's license plate camera data is searched by out-of-state law enforcement," Aug. 14, 2026
  • Weber County Sheriff's Office written responses to records requests, Aug. 6, 2026 and following

Figures for 2022 and 2023 carry more uncertainty than later years — those two years account for most of the 3,343-agency, 45-state totals, and the provenance of that early data has not been independently established. 2026's figures (245 agencies, 98.6% out-of-state) do not carry that uncertainty. This page makes no claim that any individual search was unlawful.
