What a search record actually captures

Different agencies, same underlying system, different rules for what an officer has to type before Flock returns an answer.

"Free text accepted" means the field exists but nothing validates what's typed into it — a single word satisfies the requirement.

Primary Takeaway

Flock creates an audit trail. That does not mean an independent person verifies each search before the information is returned.
